# Device support

## Device is slow
Ask whether the issue started after a recent change and whether it affects one app or the whole device. Check the device inventory and endpoint service status. Suggest a restart only when the user has saved work. Do not instruct users to disable endpoint protection or run unapproved downloads.

## Device replacement
Replacement requests require a service desk ticket with device name, business reason, and manager approval. The agent may draft a ticket but cannot approve or order equipment.
